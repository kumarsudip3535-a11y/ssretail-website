Who we are

SS Retail Services provides civil, electrical and mechanical repair and maintenance services, and builds and runs WhatsApp automation (chatbots) for businesses. This policy covers our website, online bookings, our own WhatsApp number, and WhatsApp chatbots we run for other businesses ("Clients").

When we run a chatbot for a Client (for example a restaurant, clinic or shop), that business decides why your information is collected and is responsible for it. We process it only on the Client's instructions, to provide the chatbot service.

Information we collect

When you book a service we collect: your name, mobile number, email address (optional), site type, site or outlet name, site address and city, preferred visit date, and the description of the problem you give us. When you pay, we receive the payment amount, status and Payment ID from Razorpay.

When you message us or a Client's chatbot on WhatsApp, we receive your WhatsApp profile name, phone number, the messages and button choices you send, and any images or locations you choose to share.

We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net banking password. These are handled entirely by Razorpay, our payment gateway.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and messages, including automated replies
  • To confirm your booking or order, contact you and schedule the visit or delivery
  • To pass your enquiry to the Client business you messaged, so they can serve you
  • To send messages you asked for, such as confirmations, updates, reminders and payment links
  • To prepare quotations, carry out the work and issue invoices
  • To process payments and refunds
  • To meet accounting, tax and legal requirements

We send promotional messages only if you agreed to receive them. Reply STOP at any time to stop them.

WhatsApp and Meta

Our WhatsApp services use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. Messages on WhatsApp are also covered by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. We access a business's WhatsApp Business account only with its owner's permission, and only to provide the service they asked for. We do not use WhatsApp data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Automated and AI-assisted replies

Some replies are sent automatically, and some may be written with the help of an AI service (Google Gemini) to answer common questions. Only the message text needed to write a reply is sent to that service. You can always ask to speak to a person.

Who we share it with

  • The Client business you contacted through its chatbot
  • Saanvi Consultancy Services, our booking partner that collects booking payments
  • Razorpay, to process your payment under its own privacy policy
  • Service providers that run our systems: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), Google (Google Sheets and Gemini) and Netlify (website hosting)
  • Our crew members, only as needed to attend your site
  • Government or legal authorities where the law requires it

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

WhatsApp chat and enquiry records are normally kept for up to 12 months after your last message. Booking and payment records are kept as long as needed to provide the service and to meet accounting, tax and legal requirements. After that, we delete them.

Security

Our website uses HTTPS, and payments go through Razorpay's secure checkout. Access to booking and chat details is limited to the people who need them to provide the service.

Cookies and third-party services

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and 3D graphics libraries from public content networks, and the Razorpay checkout may use its own cookies to process payments securely.

Your rights

Under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to records we must keep by law. You can withdraw consent for promotional messages at any time, and you can complain to us or to the Data Protection Board of India.
