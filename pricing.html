How pricing works

Every site is different, so the final price depends on the scope of work, the materials and parts needed, the height and access at your site, and the number of fittings or panels involved. We inspect the site and give you a written quotation before any repair work starts.

The booking advance

  • To book online, you pay an advance of ₹500 or more. You choose the amount.
  • Your booking is confirmed only after the advance payment is successful.
  • The full advance is adjusted against your final bill.
  • ₹500 of the advance covers the site visit and inspection. If you decide not to go ahead after the inspection, see our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Taxes and invoices

Quoted prices are exclusive of GST unless stated otherwise. GST is charged as applicable and a GST invoice is provided for the work.

Paying the balance

The balance amount is payable on completion of the work, as agreed in your quotation, by NEFT, RTGS, IMPS or UPI.

Online payment methods

The booking advance can be paid by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallet through Razorpay's secure checkout.

Online booking payments for SS Retail Services are collected by Saanvi Consultancy Services, our sister company and booking partner. You may see this name in your UPI app, bank statement or payment receipt.
